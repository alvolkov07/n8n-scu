Daily Briefer removes the time-consuming and draining activities that involve searching AI news and information from the internet relative to your interests. We used n8n workflows to automate this: First, we gather articles within a 7 day publishing window. Then, we use a system of GPT agents to cluster, filter out, and bundle the articles before sending them to another system of agents, which add visual diagrams and supporting information. Finally, we have GPT 5.6 Sol compose an email with formatted links that is then sent to that user at every interval of their choice. The result is an automated, agentic system that decreases the time spent browsing for relevant information about 4x.
